- Voting at equal budget: closed on this corpus. It pays only when the budget is above ~3× what one answer uses, on the two layers where a draw is below 0.9, and costs 3–5× for +4 to +12 points there.
- Cheap-swarm vs expensive-single in dollars: closed, negative, on Haiku vs Opus/Sonnet at nominal prices.
- Not closed: the single model given *more* than its natural budget (a longer-thinking setting) — no such runs exist without the cap; the budget-forcing protocol is a different operator (Q1) and cannot stand in for it.
